==Stylish Devices/Authorial Choices==

### Stylish Devices(photography)
1. **Framing**
	- Using elements within the scene to create a border around the subject, drawing attention and adding depth.
2. **Perspective**
	- The angle or viewpoint from which a photo is taken, influencing how objects are perceived.
3. **Lighting**
	- Manipulating light (natural or artificial) to set mood, highlight the subject, and define textures.
4. **Color Palette**
	- The arrangement of colors within a photo, influencing mood and visual harmony.
	- **core aspects**:
		- hue (first impression): warm, cold, grey, earth, limited
		- saturation (color intensity): saturated, desaturated, vivid, pastel
		- emotional symbolism: blue=calm/rational; black=mourning/authority; red=rage/urgency; green=renewal/plant
		- distribution: foreground, background, dominant, duality
		- contrast
5. **Composition**
	- The placement of elements in an image, guiding the viewer's eye and creating balance.
6. **Portraiture**
	- Photography focused on capturing the personality and emotion of a person or group.
7. **Subject Selection**
	- Choosing the main focus of the image to tell a story or emphasize a message.

### Authorial Choices
1. **Narrative Storytelling**
   - The technique of structuring and presenting a story through plot, characters, and themes to engage the audience.
2. **Social Activities**
   - Interactions or events between individuals or groups that reflect societal dynamics or relationships in a narrative.
3. **Culture Preservation**
   - The effort to maintain and convey cultural traditions, practices, and values through creative works or storytelling.
4. **Artistic Vision**
   - The unique creative perspective and intent that guides an author’s style, themes, and message in their work.
