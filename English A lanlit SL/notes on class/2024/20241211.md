> **Salem witchhunt trials** and **McCarthyism Blacklist**, how they assemble and connect to 21st century?

Although against different groups of people, both served the purpose of **reinforcement of conformity** within the community by creating common social enemy, as a mean to strengthen current power structure and social control. In **Salem witchhunt trials**, the initial victims were marginalized people who were accused of witchcraft. While for **McCarthyism Blacklist**, it prosecuted left-wing individual and communists. Both spread fear to the public of these enemy, causing social instability and thus making the society and public opinion easier to control.
