Two things I learnt that I think are the most important
1. **denotation -> connotation process**: This process helps us to better analyze the text, no matter the type. This standardized method makes our analysis more organized and more logical.
2. **terminologies** for slogan, photo, posters, etc. By using these terminlogies, we can be more precise and looks more professional to analyze text or artworks. It also allows us to look at the text in different aspects to get a complete view.

**Action verbs** matter. concise and precise. examples:
- The cat ~~went~~ leaped to the window.
- She ~~looked~~ stared/behold at the painting.
- He ~~made~~ baked a cake for the party.
- They ~~talked about~~ fabricated their plans.
- The teacher ~~gave~~ confused/murdered/assassinated the students with assignments.

**Concrete details** to create imagery. Examples:
- ~~The project is going well.~~ The project's process made everyone smile.
- They will meet soon. 
- The results were good.
- ~~He is a nice person.~~ He saved a kitten in a storm
- The weather is bad today.
