**Framing and Composition:**  
The expansive framing places the subjects within a vast natural landscape, connecting them to their cultural roots and origin.

The balanced composition(placing half human half nature) with horizontal lines create a metaphor for continuity between human experience and the environment, and thus reflect harmony between human and nature.

**Lighting and Color Palette:**  
The warm tones of the rising sun create a sense of spirituality and peace through the color of orange, pink, and bluish gray.

The vibrant traditional clothing stands out against the hued natural surroundings, emphasizing the unique identity of their culture.

**Portraiture and Subject Selection:**  
The subjects’ facial expressions and posture show wisdom and quiet strength. Two individual were all gazing at something that is far away.

These highlight the passing down of knowledge, cultural values, and hope for the future.

In addition, the elder was holding the child's hand. This symbolizes the protective and nurturing role of the elder in passing down traditions.

**Symbolism and Metaphor:**  
The dawn background and the child symbolize the hope of the culture to be pass down forever and the renewal for the culture's future. (the dawn = a new day)

**Emotional and Imaginative Impact:**  
The image encourages a deeper appreciation of the subjects' cultural identity and beliefs, inviting reflection on art, creativity, and storytelling.
