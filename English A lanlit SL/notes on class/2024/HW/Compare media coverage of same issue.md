Both pieces are about the Chinese Government slams the new chip export policy by US.

The key difference between the two pieces lie in their attitude: the CNN is pro-US, while the CZTV is pro-China.

In terms of the framing of the two pieces, they have different focus. The CZTV focuses more on the Chinese response to the policy, which blames US for 'broadening the concept of national safety' and the negative impact on global supply chain. The press especially mentioned the violation of international contracts of international organization, like WTO and WSC. For CNN, it focuses more on the reason why the US restricted the chip export to China, especially mentioning the Chinese military usage of chips and its threat to US and its allies.

The use of language in both pieces reflect the attitude towards the restriction policy. For CZTV, the words used, like 'violation', 'excuse', 'abuse', etc., portrayed US as a game breaker violating rules. For CNN, it used phrases like 'stealing American-made AI software', which illustrates China as a thief and justifies the policy of limiting chip export to China.
