### Kosovo Conflict Causes (short term)

**Political causes**

**1. Constitutional change (1989–1994)**

- 1974: Kosovo = autonomous within Yugoslavia
    
- 1989: Milošević **revokes autonomy** → Belgrade control (police, courts, education)
    
- Albanians excluded from state institutions
    

**2. Albanian response**

- 1990: declare **Republic of Kosovo** (unrecognized)
    
- 1991: independence referendum
    
- 1992–94: **parallel state** (schools, healthcare, taxes)
    

**3. Repression**

- Mass job dismissals (Albanians)
    
- Albanian-language education restricted
    
- Police violence and surveillance  
    → Shift: autonomy demands → **independence movement**
    

---

**Key figures**

**Slobodan Milošević**

- Serbian leader using **nationalism (Kosovo issue)**
    
- Drives 1989 reforms → centralization
    
- Repression escalates conflict  
    → Turns political dispute into **ethnic conflict**
    

**Ibrahim Rugova**

- Leader of LDK
    
- Strategy: **non-violent resistance + parallel state**
    
- Gains international sympathy
    
- Fails to stop repression  
    → Weakening support → rise of armed groups (KLA)
    

---

**Core dynamic**  
Milošević repression → Rugova peaceful resistance → failure → radicalization
