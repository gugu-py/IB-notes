## Social impact

### woman

since the majority of victims in the genocide were men, women became central in social reconstruction and economic development

66% households headed by women; two thirds of the parliaments were women; gender right included in constitution

### refugee

UNHCR aimed to restart refugee's life

challenges in refugee camps:
- extremists and killers have strong influence/ retained control over large population in the refugee camps
- food/aid distribution, deal with Interhamwe
- sorting out killers from refugees


## Justice and Reconciliation

Establish of ICTR (International Crime Tribunal for Rwanda)

Low efficiency against the participants: By 1998, total prison population 130,000, but 1,200 bought before courts

Gacaca Courts: people from local community were elected as judges to try cases before the peers.
- 2 million cases in ten years
- challenge: neighbors turned on each other; spoken witness being murdered in some cases

## Political Impact

RPF gaining power after 1994

stressed nationalism "Rwandaness", abolished ethnicity discrimintation

RPF controlled the mechanism over:
- media, state beuracracy, bank, security service, etc.

## Economic Impact

45% people in poverty, less than 1.5$ per day

strong reliance on agriculture
- 75% people tied to agriculture, coffee, and tea; 80% of export
