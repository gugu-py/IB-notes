## Scramble for Africa

### Explanation for imperialism

#### 1 Economic forces

- ==Hobson== suggested the **shrinking of markets and surplus of capital in need of markets**.
- ==Lenin== adapted his idea: Imperialism was "a direct continuation of the fundamental properties of capitalism in general". 

#### 2 Social forces

- The prevailing of ==Social Darwinism==, developed by ==Herbert Spencer==.
- ==Racial hierarchy== and ==Race Theory==

#### 3 Strategic and military forces

The need to keep ahead of rivals in the drive to secure empire. For example, Britain.

#### 4 Individuals
They did this through independent actions on the ground, making deals with local rulers and by refusing or “misinterpreting" orders from their governments.

examples: 
Frenchman Pierre Savorgnan de Brazza, the GermanKarl Peters, Welshman Henry Morton Stanley or military men such as Charles"Chinese" Gordon
