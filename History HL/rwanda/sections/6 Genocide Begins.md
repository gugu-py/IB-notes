Genocide took place in different places, rather than in Kigali alone.

An ==estimated 200,000 Hutu participated== in the genocide; some joined in gladly and killed for pleasure and gain; some were unwilling, threatened and killed for not complying with what others were doing around them.

**Killing method** was brutal:
- clubs, machetes(most common), spears, crude instruments
- Not killed instantly (disabled first, and killed another day)
- Rape -> spread of HIV

## **media**

The UN and NGOs had warned of the central role the radio played in inciting ethnic tensions and encouraging murder. The UNAMIR commander ==Romeo Dallaire== urged for **RTLM to be taken off the air** but the UN itself did nothave the means to stop the radio station broadcasting either through jamming, direct air strikes or covert means.
