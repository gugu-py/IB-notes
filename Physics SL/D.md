point mass/gravitational field
- assumption of gravitational force: The objects are point mass.

Kepler first law: Each planet orbits the Sun along an elliptical path. The Sun is located at a focus point of the elliptical orbit.

Kepler second law: Joining the line between the planet and the sun, the area swept is the same in equal time

Kepler third law: $\text{period}^2 \propto \text{distance}_{\text{between the sun and the planet (average)}}^3$ 


SI unit of gravitational field strength = acceleration ($m/s^2$)
