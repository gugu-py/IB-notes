### extended binomial expansion

for $(a+b)^k, \\ k \in R$ :expansion:

$$
(a+b)^k
=a^k(1+\frac{b}{a})^k=a^k \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \binom{k}{n} (\frac{b}{a})^n
$$
to converge, satisfy: $|\frac{b}{a}|<1$

### Polynomial Division

for two polynomial $g(x)$ and $f(x)$, we have $\frac{g(x)}{f(x)}$.
If degree of $g(x)$ is bigger than $f(x)$, use polynomial division

### Partial Fraction

for two polynomial $g(x)$ and $f(x)$, we have $\frac{g(x)}{f(x)}$.
If degree of $g(x)$ is smaller than $f(x)$, use Partial Fraction

ensure $f(x)$ is fully factorized 

for $f(x)$, if it has form of:
1. $(x+a_0)$: $\frac{A}{x+a_0}$
2. $(x+a_0)^n$: $\frac{A_1}{(x+a_0)}+\frac{A_2}{(x+a_0)^2}+...+\frac{A_n}{(x+a_0)^n}$
3. $(ax^2+bx+c)$: use factor of $\frac{Ax+B}{(ax^2+bx+c)}$
